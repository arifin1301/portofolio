About Me

Who I Am

I'm Dion Arifin, a multidisciplinary creative developer based in Indonesia. With expertise spanning web design, development, and data analysis, I build digital solutions that are both beautiful and functional.

My recent projects include SinikitAanuin Agency (a creative digital agency platform), Nails by DNNA (a beauty booking platform), KNN Financial Planner (an AI-powered financial tool), and DompetKu (a personal finance dashboard).

I believe in creating work that not only looks great but drives real results through data-driven insights and user-centered design.
